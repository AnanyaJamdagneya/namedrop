THE RULES

How to play

  1. Split into teams. Sit so teammates alternate around the circle.
  2. One deck, every round. The same cards come back each round, so pay attention. You'll want to remember them.
  3. On your turn, one player gives clues to their own team until the timer runs out. Tap Got it or Skip. Skipped cards go back in the pile.
  4. Rounds get harder:
    1 Say anything: any words except the name itself.
    2 One word: exactly one word per card. Guessers get unlimited guesses.
    3 Act it out: charades. No words, sound effects allowed.
    4 Bonus, sound only: eyes closed for guessers, noises only. (optional)
  5. Clear the deck mid-turn and your leftover seconds carry into the next round, and your team goes first.
  6. Points are printed on each card. Harder names are worth more. Highest total after the last round wins.

Keyboard: Space/→ got it · ←/S skip · P pause
